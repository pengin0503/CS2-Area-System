# CS2 Area System

[日本語](README.md) | **English**

A Cities: Skylines II 1.6.2f1 mod adding managed Park, Industrial and Tourism areas. Park and Industrial areas observe the game; Tourism is boundary-only and has no tourism gameplay yet.

## Use

Enable a mod build made with the official toolchain. Open **Areas → Area Types**, select a type and draw a boundary. Then select the completed area via **Managed areas** at the top right. Its panel shows name, location, type, status and progress and lets you edit or delete it.

- **Park:** assign a Park Maintenance facility inside the boundary as headquarters, and provide a dedicated pedestrian Path crossing from outside to inside or a supported entrance. Ordinary road sidewalks do not count as automatic entrances.
- **Industrial:** assign an ordinary office building inside the boundary as headquarters.
- **Tourism:** boundary management only.

Click an eligible facility during assignment. Under **Options → CS2 Area System**, configure overlap, panel position, deletion confirmation and other behavior.

## Park and Industrial areas

Parks show visitors, quality, landscape, finances and progress. Budgets, admission fees and an event are available. Visitor changes depend on game conditions; the mod does not directly change citizens' destination choices.

Industrial areas show eligible companies' production, logistics, employment, efficiency, bottlenecks and progress. Unavailable metrics are shown as unavailable rather than zero. Industrial level itself grants no production, efficiency, tax or demand bonus.
